Privacy Policy

Orneo World Automation Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Overview

Orneo World Automation ("the App") uploads videos to a TikTok account that the user has authorized, using TikTok's official Content Posting API. This policy explains what data the App handles.

2. Information we handle

  • TikTok authorization data: the access token, refresh token and Open ID issued by TikTok after you log in and grant permission.
  • Content you provide: video files, captions and hashtags that you choose to publish.
  • Basic operational records: publish status and timestamps, used to track whether a post succeeded.

We do not collect your TikTok password. Login is handled entirely by TikTok.

3. How we use information

Data is used only to publish the content you submit to your own TikTok account and to show you the result of each post. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Data is shared only with TikTok (to publish your content) and with the infrastructure providers needed to run the App (for example, cloud storage for your video files). These providers process data only to operate the service.

5. Storage and retention

Authorization tokens and publishing records are stored securely and kept only as long as needed to operate the App. Video files are kept only until they are published, unless you choose to keep them in your own storage.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect data, including restricting access to credentials. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services).
  • To request deletion of any data we hold, contact us at the email below and we will delete it promptly.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
